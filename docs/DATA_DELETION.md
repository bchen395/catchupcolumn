# Data Deletion Request — Catch Up Column

## Delete your account from the app (fastest)

1. Open Catch Up Column and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Tap **Delete my account**, at the bottom.
3. Confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately.

This permanently deletes:

- your profile (display name, photo, bio, email);
- all posts and photos you created;
- your uploaded images in storage;
- your memberships in every group. Each group carries on for its other members: if
  you're its only moderator, the member who joined it earliest becomes its
  moderator, and a group with no one else in it is deleted.

## Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email `support@catchupcolumn.com` from the email
address on your account with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will verify
you own the address and delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

## What we retain

Once deletion completes we do not retain your profile or content. Transient
operational logs that may contain your email are rotated automatically and are not
used to reconstruct your account. If you wrote to us or reported a story, we keep
that email in our support inbox for as long as we need it to help you.
