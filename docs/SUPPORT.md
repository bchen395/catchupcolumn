# Catch Up Column — Support

Need a hand? We're happy to help.

**Email:** `support@catchupcolumn.com`
We usually reply within 2 business days. Reports of abusive or objectionable
content are read within 24 hours, every day.

## Common questions

**How do I join a group?**
Ask the group's creator for their invite code, then open the app and tap
**Groups → Join** and enter the code.

**When do editions come out?**
Each group has its own publish day and time, set by its moderator. When an edition
publishes, everyone in the group gets it in the app, by email, and by push
notification.

**I stopped getting edition emails / I get too many.**
Open the group in the app. Under **Your delivery**, **Email me each edition** turns
that group's emails on or off, and **Notify me when it's out** does the same for its
notification. Every edition email also has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.
Whatever you choose, every edition is still in the app's **Editions** tab.

**How do I report a story, or stop seeing someone's?**
Tap **Report this story** at the foot of it, or email us — we read every report
within 24 hours. **Block** in the same place hides that person's stories from you;
unblock from the group's page.

**How do I delete a post?**
Open the compose screen for that group before the edition publishes — you can edit
or delete your post there. Once an edition is published, ask your group's moderator
to take it down.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: **Profile → Delete my account**. This permanently removes your profile,
your posts and photos, and your group memberships. If you can't access the app, see
[Data Deletion Request](./DATA_DELETION.md).
