Privacy Policy

Effective July 3, 2026

Last updated November 2, 2026

Catch Up Column ("the app", "we", "us") is a private group newsletter you write together with family and friends. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Who this app is for

Catch Up Column is intended for adults (18+) and the family members and friends they invite. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work:

Account information

  • Email address — for sign-in (we email you a one-time code), confirming it's you before a password is set, telling you when your password changes, and delivering your group's editions.
  • Password, only if you choose to set one from your profile — stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider; we never see it. Every account can sign in with an emailed code, and a password is never required.
  • Display name — shown as your byline to other members of your groups.
  • Profile photo (optional) and short bio (optional).
  • Safety records — the people you've blocked (only you can see them), people removed from a group you moderate (only its moderators can see them), when you agreed to our Terms, and reports you send us (your note and email address).

Content you create

  • Posts (title and body text) and any photos you attach.
  • Groups you create (name, description, optional cover image) and your membership in groups.

Device information for notifications

  • A push-notification token and your device platform (iOS/Android), so we can tell you when a new edition is ready. You can decline or revoke this in your device settings.

Automatically recorded

  • Timestamps for accounts, posts, and editions (when they were created or published).

Crash reports

  • When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry, our crash-reporting provider. It contains the error and its stack trace, your device model, OS version, and app version. It does not contain your name, email, posts, photos, or any identifier that links the report back to you, and we have turned off performance tracking and session recording.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing activity. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind across apps or websites. Apart from the SDKs of the providers that run the app (Supabase and Expo, below), crash reporting, described above, is the only third-party SDK in the app, and it exists so we can fix what breaks.

How your content is shared

Catch Up Column is private by design. Your posts and photos are visible only to members of the specific group you post them in. Editions are delivered only to the members of that group. There is no public feed and no discovery of your content by strangers. If someone reports a story, we read it to act on the report.

Service providers (data processors)

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the app. They process your data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (your account, content, and photos are stored here). supabase.com/privacy
  • Resend — sends your sign-in and confirmation codes, account security notices, and the weekly edition emails, and delivers the reports you send us from the app. When we email you a code or a notice, your email address is sent to Resend to deliver it; when an edition publishes, the recipient email addresses and the edition content are sent to Resend for delivery; when you report a story, your note and email address are sent through Resend to reach us. resend.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Sentry — receives the crash reports described above, and nothing else. sentry.io/privacy
  • Expo / Expo Application Services — delivers push notifications and app updates. Your push token and the notification text (e.g. your group's name) pass through Expo's push service. When the app checks for an update, it sends Expo a random identifier for your installation of the app, which isn't tied to your account. expo.dev/privacy
  • Apple and Google deliver the notifications to your device through their push networks.
  • Cloudflare — forwards email sent to support@catchupcolumn.com, including the reports you send from the app, to our support inbox. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

How long we keep your data

We keep your account and content until you delete them. Deleting a post removes it. Deleting your account removes your profile and content as described below. When you write to us or report a story, we keep that email in our support inbox for as long as we need it to help you.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the app: Profile → Delete my account. This removes your profile, your posts and photos, your uploaded images, and your group memberships. Your groups carry on for their other members: if you're a group's only moderator, the member who joined it earliest becomes its moderator, and a group with no one else in it is deleted.

If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion by emailing support@catchupcolumn.com from the email address on your account. See Data Deletion Request.

Your choices

  • Edition emails: turn a group's edition emails off or back on from that group's page in the app. Every edition email also includes a one-tap unsubscribe link, per group.
  • Push notifications: turn them off for one group from that group's page in the app, or for the whole app in your device settings.
  • Profile: edit your display name, photo, and bio in the app.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your content is enforced by per-group access rules so that only members of a group can read its posts and editions. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@catchupcolumn.com
